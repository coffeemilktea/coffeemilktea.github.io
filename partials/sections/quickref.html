Start from the task rather than the format — most integration problems land in one of these.

  • A message came through wrong and I need to see why HL7 v2.x Parser
  • Two messages should match, but one failed HL7 Diff Checker
  • I need to look at a study without a PACS viewer DICOM Viewer & Tag Morph
  • A header is malformed and needs correcting DICOM Viewer & Tag Morph
  • I need test files that don't contain real patients DICOM Toolbox
  • A modality can't pull its worklist MWL Simulator
  • I have images but the order message is missing DICOM → HL7 Order
